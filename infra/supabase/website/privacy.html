Where this page refers to the "Service", it means the PocketPass application for Android (the "App"), the website at pocketpass.xyz (the "Website"), the developer portal at developer.pocketpass.xyz and the account, messaging, encounter and API services that support them.

Part A. Privacy Policy

A1. Controller and contact

The Service is operated by the PocketPass team (referred to in this Policy as "PocketPass", "we", "us" or "our"), an independent group of individuals and not a commercial company. We are the controller of the personal data described in this Policy. The Service is hosted on a single cloud server rented from Oracle Cloud and administered by us. Enquiries concerning this Policy, including requests to exercise the rights described in section A9, may be submitted through the PocketPass Discord server.

A2. Categories of personal data processed

Account data. There are three ways to hold an account. If you sign in with an email address, we store that address; sign-in uses a six-digit code that expires ten minutes after it is issued and no password is involved. If you sign in with Discord, we store the Discord account identifier and display name supplied by Discord. If you create a username account, we store the username you chose and a salted hash of your password produced by the authentication service; no email address is held for such an account, and internally it is identified by a reserved address of the form username@users.pocketpass.xyz that receives no mail. Because no address is on file, a forgotten password cannot be reset by you or by us. You may later link a verified email address to a username account from Settings, after which that address is stored as described above and the account may also sign in with a code sent to it. For every account we record the date and time of registration and of your most recent sign-in.

Profile data. Your display name, username, biography, Piip designs (comprising the cosmetic parameters and the rendered portrait image), an optional age, and the country you selected during setup. Age and country are displayed to other users exactly as you entered them. The App does not access the location services of your device; the country shown is solely the one you selected. We also record an approximate "last active" timestamp so that your friends can see whether you are available.

Social data. Your friend code, your friendships, pending friend requests sent and received, and the users you have blocked.

Messages. The text of your conversations, images you attach, replies, edit history, and read status. Deleting a message replaces its text with the words "Message deleted" for every participant. Attached images are held in private storage that can be opened only by members of the conversation concerned.

Boards. We store board proposals, membership and invitations, board names and artwork, typed and drawn notes, replies, Yeah reactions, reports, appeals, moderation decisions, and stationery entitlements. Public boards are readable by signed-in PocketPass users; private boards are readable by their members and authorised reviewers. Drawings are stored as strokes with generated previews. Imported board artwork is resized and has image metadata removed. Drafts autosave on your device and sync to owner-private cloud storage when online; they are never posted automatically. Conflicting draft versions are kept separately.

Encounter data. The identity of each user you have encountered, the time of the encounter, and whether both parties confirmed it. Encounter records are retained for the lifetime of your account because World Tour, the leaderboards and several achievements are calculated from them.

Activity data. Your token balance, shop purchases, achievements, Bingo progress and supporter status. If you enable Step Rewards on Android and grant Health Connect read access, the App reads today's step records on your device, excludes manually entered and unverified records, and uses the device step sensor when Health Connect is unavailable or inaccessible. On iOS, the App uses the device pedometer. The App transmits only a daily step total for the current day and the previous day, never raw Health Connect records, source-app details or sensor readings, and only for as long as the setting remains enabled. You can revoke Health Connect access in Android settings.

Notifications. In-app notifications are retained for 90 days and are then deleted automatically. If you enable message alerts, we register a notification token for your device and associate it with your account and sign-in session. Google Firebase Cloud Messaging delivers these alerts, using Apple Push Notification service on iOS. Android push payloads contain routing identifiers, the sender or group name, and a short message preview. iOS push payloads contain routing identifiers and a generic new-message alert, without the sender name or message text. You can turn message alerts off in the App or in your device settings. Joined-board activity alerts are enabled by default, with separate board push controls and per-board mute settings. Board push payloads contain routing identifiers and a generic activity notice, without note text, drawings, or board names.

Connected applications and developer data. The third-party applications you have authorised to access your account and the permissions you granted to each. If you register an application in the developer portal, we hold the details of that application and a count of the requests it makes per day.

Technical data. Our servers keep ordinary access logs, which include IP addresses, for a short period for the purposes of troubleshooting and security. The Website sets no cookies, contains no analytics or tracking code, and keeps no record of its visitors beyond those server logs. The App contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting components.

A3. Nearby Encounters

Nearby Encounters is disabled until you enable it. While it is enabled, the Bluetooth radio of your device broadcasts only a protocol version number and a random identifier that changes frequently. Your name, Piip, account identifier and all other personal data are never broadcast.

When two devices running the App come within range, they establish an encrypted connection and exchange single-use passes issued by our server. Passes expire seven days after issue and are deleted from our systems approximately one day after they are used. The cryptographic keys involved never leave your device, and the records of the exchange itself are discarded together with the passes. An encounter, and the resulting disclosure of your profile to the other user, comes into existence only once both users have confirmed it in the App.

On Android 11 the operating system requires the location permission in order to scan for Bluetooth devices. The App requests that permission on Android 11 for that purpose alone and does not read your position. On Android 12 and later the App uses the dedicated Bluetooth permissions instead.

We do not use personal data for advertising, we do not build profiles for marketing purposes, and we do not sell personal data.

A5. Recipients of personal data

Other users. Your profile data is visible to users who are permitted to view your profile, such as your friends and users with whom you have a confirmed encounter. Messages are visible to the participants in the conversation. Board content is shared with the audience described above. Blocking hides board content between the accounts involved; authorised moderation review can still access it.

Service providers. Resend delivers the sign-in and verification code emails and therefore processes your email address whenever an address is held for your account. If you choose Discord sign-in, we receive your Discord identity and display name. Our private Discord staff alert for Ko-fi payments contains a payment identifier, amount and matching account identifier or status, but not the payer's email address or name. Ko-fi sends us a notice for each payment containing the payer's email address, name, tier, amount and any message so that the associated benefits can be applied to the account registered with the same email address or to the account whose username or friend code appears in the message. We retain the payer email in a restricted matching field, not a second copy in the raw notice. Payment records may remain after an account is deleted. A username account without an email address receives benefits when the payer names it in the message, or once it links the email address used at Ko-fi. GitHub hosts the App's release files, and your device downloads updates directly from GitHub, which receives the download request like any other. Pretendo Network is contacted only when you import a Mii, and receives only the Pretendo Network ID you enter.

Third-party applications. An application you connect to your account receives only the data covered by the permissions you approved on the consent screen. Your email address is shared only if you explicitly approve the OpenID email permission; the app can then receive it in an identity token or from the UserInfo service. Other connected-app permissions and ordinary profile requests do not include email, and connected apps do not receive your Discord identity. Board content and private cloud drafts require their separate board permissions. You may review and revoke every connected application at links.pocketpass.xyz/oauth/apps or in the App under Settings.

Legal disclosures. We may disclose personal data where we are required to do so by law or by a binding order of a competent authority.

The App uses Firebase Cloud Messaging for message and board alerts. Android delivery uses Google Play services; iOS delivery uses Apple Push Notification service. These providers process the device token and push payload to deliver notifications. This integration does not enable Firebase Analytics, and the App does not include an advertising component or CAPTCHA service.

A6. Storage, security and international transfer

Our account, profile, social, message, encounter and activity records are stored in a database on a single cloud server rented from Oracle Cloud and administered by us. Notification delivery additionally sends the device token and push payload described in section A2 through Google Firebase and, on iOS, Apple; those providers may process delivery data outside the server's country. Connections to the server and from our authentication service to our email provider are encrypted. Onward delivery to a recipient's mail server follows the provider's normal policy and is not guaranteed to be encrypted end to end. Account email remains readable to the authentication service for sign-in and email delivery; other users cannot retrieve it through profiles. The cloud provider encrypts server volumes at rest, and we encrypt backup archives separately. Access controls ensure that each account can read only its own data and data that has been shared with it. Encrypted backups are taken nightly; seven days of backups are kept on the server and one further copy is kept off the server. If you access the Service from a country other than the one in which the server is located, your personal data will be transferred to and processed in that location.

A7. Retention

Account, profile, social, message, encounter and activity data are retained for as long as your account exists. In-app notifications are retained for 90 days. Queued push alerts expire after 15 minutes, and device registrations expire after 30 days without refresh. Turning an alert category off disables that category when the App can reach the server; the registration may remain for other enabled alerts. Signing out clears the local notification binding immediately and attempts to remove the server registration and Firebase token. A generic iOS alert already queued before an offline logout may still arrive, but its tap cannot open another account's conversation. Passes are retained for no more than seven days after issue and for approximately one day after use. Server access logs are retained for a short period only. Ko-fi payment notices are retained as payment records for as long as is necessary to account for the payment. Except for the board moderation retention described below, data you delete is removed from live systems immediately but may remain within encrypted backups for up to approximately seven days before those backups are overwritten.

Removed board content and previous text versions are retained for 30 days, or longer while an associated report or appeal remains open. Resolved word-filter review records are kept for 30 days. Current cloud draft versions remain until discarded, published, or the board is permanently deleted; superseded draft versions expire after 30 days. Permanent staff deletion of a board purges its content, drafts, reports, and assets from live storage, retaining only a minimal action audit. Backup copies follow the backup retention above.

A8. Administrative access

To operate and support the Service, members of the PocketPass team may look up an account (including its email address or username), correct token balances and achievements, and link a Ko-fi payment to an account. Every such action is recorded in an audit trail. No other person has this account-administration access.

Board owners and moderators review their board's reports, membership, and appeals. Reports concerning board staff go to PocketPass staff. Reporter identity is not shown to the reported person. PocketPass staff need a separate permission to inspect private boards, and review access and moderation actions are logged. Cloud drafts are not exposed through board-review access. Staff-configured literal word filters apply to new board text and edits: rules either reject the submission with a reason or replace matching text and retain the original for staff review. Matching biography edits are rejected while preserving your last accepted bio and attempted draft. Private messages are outside this filter. Boards do not use automated image or OCR moderation.
