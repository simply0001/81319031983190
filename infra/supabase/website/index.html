Take a look

Your profile

Put a face to the people you pass. Your profile introduces you to others nearby, and the people you've met stay here so you can find them again.

Conversations

Keep the conversation going after you've met. Send a quick message, share a photo or bring everyone together in a group chat.

Friends

Add the people you want to stay in touch with, see who's around and send a wave to say hello.

Games and rewards

Play a round of Bingo, collect puzzle pieces and see how you're doing on the leaderboard. The tokens you earn can go towards a new hat for your Piip.

Settings

Make PocketPass feel right for you, from its look and sounds to the notifications you receive. This is also where you manage your account and nearby encounters.

Your Piip

Make a Piip that looks like you, or try something completely different. Pick its features, play with colours and save a few favourites to switch between.

How it works

How passes work

The idea is the same as StreetPass on the Nintendo 3DS.

1

Your phone holds a few passes

The app keeps a few single-use passes on your phone. Your data is completely private this way.

2

Passing someone spends one pass

Two devices with PocketPass in Bluetooth LE range automatically send encrypted payloads to eachother using these passes, which means your device identifier data is never compromised

3

Both of your devices confirm

Once both of your devices confirm the pass, your Piips now show on eachothers devices!

Passes are anonymous until both sides confirm, and encrypted so they cannot be copied or forged.

Avatars

Piips

Your Piip is the avatar other people see when you pass them.

Make one in the editor, keep a few in slots, or import a Mii with a Pretendo Network ID. Hats cost tokens in the shop, and a Ko-fi membership unlocks all of them for 36 days per payment, matched by your Ko-fi email or the account named in the message.

Developers

Developer API

Sites and apps can connect to PocketPass accounts.

Sign users in with PocketPass and use the API for profiles, friends, messages, notifications, tokens and encounters, with live updates. People see what an app asks for and can disconnect it at any time.
