PocketPass Boards is here! Open Messages and choose Boards to find communities, write posts, and share drawn notes. You can request your own public or private board for staff approval.

Draw with pixel or smooth pens, reply to notes, and give them a Yeah. Drafts save automatically and sync between your devices. You choose when to post them.

Boards includes invitations, membership requests, spoilers, activity notifications, and tools for owners and moderators. Each board has its own mute and push settings.

Also included:
- A Block Messages setting that stops new direct messages and group invitations while keeping your existing groups.
- A leaderboard setting to show 20, 50, 75, or 100 people.
- Matching message-list colours and a new DS-style crying emoji.

This is an Android alpha release. iOS distribution will come later.
