Boards has a new home! Open Messages to go straight to Boards, with private chats, activity, and drafts close by. The refreshed layout gives drawings more room, adds useful previews on Thor's top screen, and brings a new Boards music track.

Board activity now tells you who posted or replied and shows a short preview. Open takes you to the note or report it is about. You can open a member's profile from Boards and send a friend request there. Board invitations no longer list friends who have already joined, and you can invite someone using their eight-digit Friend Code.

The Shop now opens with categories, so hats have the whole screen when you browse them. Wear puts a hat on your active Mii without leaving the Shop, and the item shows Wearing once it is equipped.

Also included:
- Block Messages now prevents new friend requests too, while leaving existing friendships and groups in place.
- The controller highlight stays visible and moves smoothly when opening or leaving Games, Shop, and Leaderboard. It also returns to Buy a piece after a Puzzle Swap purchase.
- The keyboard has swapped the positions of Caps Lock and the numbers key.
- Connected apps can use Boards and blocking features when you grant the requested permissions.
